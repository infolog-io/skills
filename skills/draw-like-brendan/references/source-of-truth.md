# Source of truth

## Authority

1. Original drawings supplied by Brendan for the current task.
2. Original Brendan drawings in `assets/source-art/`.
3. User-approved original additions whose manifest entry does not set `style_authority: false`.
4. Crops made directly from those originals.

Generated images, reconstructions, “closer” examples, contact sheets, and previous outputs have no style authority. They may describe structure only. In the project manifest, `kind: "structure"` or `style_authority: false` is a hard exclusion from style selection.

## What the originals actually establish

- **Composition:** usually one visual proposition, not a complete information architecture. Empty space is active. Scale is blunt and often funny.
- **Drawing:** objects are constructed from a few crude shapes. They are recognizable because the choices are specific, not because the contours are polished.
- **Lettering:** letters are drawings. Width, height, spacing, baseline, counters, and repeated glyphs vary visibly.
- **Color:** hard flat fills; black or white grounds are common, but saturated red, green, cyan, yellow, blue, pink, and purple grounds are equally native when the chosen source uses them.
- **Tone:** terse, literal, strange, dry, and useful. One good visual joke beats a dozen explanatory icons.

## Selection rule

Select 2–4 individual originals by analogy:

- one for the overall composition;
- one for the main object or relationship;
- one for lettering when text matters;
- optionally one for palette.

Do not lead with a contact sheet. Do not attach the whole archive. If the closest source is a three-circle Venn diagram, borrow its scale and placement for another three-part relationship; do not borrow a nine-card board because the topic is “technical.”

## Pass question

Ask: “Did this new idea inherit a compositional decision from a specific Brendan original, or did the model invent a generic diagram and add Brendan-ish marks?” Only the first can pass.
