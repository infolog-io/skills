# SkillOpt — Score History

Historical grader scores for the skills in `../../skills/`. Two sources:

- **Optimize / sweep runs** — generated from `runs/<skill>/<timestamp>/cost.json`.
  Regenerate with `python3 scores.py` after any run.
- **Eval runs** (test-split scoring, no edits) — logged by hand below; the
  harness does not persist a `cost.json` for `eval`.

Means are in `[0,1]`. `Δ val` = best minus incumbent on the val split.
`Accepted epochs` = epochs that beat the incumbent and were checkpointed.

> Note: runs before 2026-06-01 show `accepted_epochs: 0` because of the
> counter bug fixed later in `harness.py`; their val/test means are still valid.

## How to update

- After `optimize` / `sweep`: `cd tools/skillopt && python3 scores.py` —
  refreshes the generated table from the run dirs.
- After `eval`: add one row to the Eval log by hand (date, skill, split,
  mean, per-task, cost, one-line note).
